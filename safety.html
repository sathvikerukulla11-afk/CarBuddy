Safety Center

Trusted community transportation

CarBuddy exists so people who already share a school, a neighbourhood, or a team can coordinate trips they're taking anyway. It is not a service for arranging rides between strangers, and it is emphatically not a way for children to get into cars with adults nobody knows. Everything below is how we keep it that way.

✓

Verified accounts

Members can request verification, which an administrator reviews by hand. Verification status is printed on every ride card, request, and profile — you always know who you're dealing with.

🚘

Driver checks

A driver's rating, completed-ride count, and verification badge travel with every ride they post. CarBuddy does not employ, license, insure, or background-check drivers — it is a coordination tool, not a taxi company, and we say so plainly.

👪

Parent / guardian approval

Riders under 18 cannot join or post any ride until an adult is linked to their account. After that, every single request pauses for that adult's decision — the driver's Accept button is disabled by the database itself until approval lands. A young rider cannot unlink their guardian.

👥

Trusted groups

Restrict a ride to a real community: a school, a team, a neighbourhood. Group members are approved by a group admin, and a group-only ride is invisible to everyone outside it.

📍

Public meetup points

We ask for a public, well-lit place instead of a home address, and the meetup details are released only after a seat is confirmed. Private addresses never appear on a public listing.

📞

Contact details on a need-to-know basis

Phone numbers are held in a separate, locked-down table. They are released only to people confirmed on the same ride and to a linked guardian — never on a profile or a search result.

📤

Share your ride

Every ride has a shareable link. Send it to a parent or a friend so somebody who isn't travelling knows the route, the time, and who is driving.

⭐

Ratings that can't be gamed

Only people who actually shared a ride that was marked completed can rate each other, and only once per ride. The database enforces both rules, so a rating average cannot be inflated or brigaded.

🚫

Report and block

Block someone and you vanish from each other's search results entirely — neither of you can see or join the other's rides. Reports go to a moderation queue that administrators work through.

If something goes wrong

Right now, during a ride

  • If anyone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first. CarBuddy is not a monitoring service and cannot dispatch help.
  • Ask to be let out somewhere public — a shop, a petrol station, a school.
  • Call the person you shared your ride link with.

Afterwards

  • Report the member from their profile or the ride page. Include dates and details.
  • Block them so neither of you appears to the other again.
  • If a minor was involved, tell their linked guardian — they can see the ride record.

Community rules

These apply to everyone. Breaking them gets an account suspended.

Drivers

  • Only post trips you were genuinely taking anyway. This is not a job.
  • Offer only seats that exist, with a working seatbelt each.
  • Hold a valid licence and insurance for the vehicle you're driving.
  • Never drive under the influence of anything, ever.
  • Keep any contribution to a fair share of fuel. Cash in person only.
  • Never take a rider anywhere other than the destination you posted.

Riders

  • Be honest about your age. It changes what protections apply to you.
  • Turn up on time at the agreed public meetup point.
  • Cancel early if plans change — someone else may want the seat.
  • Pay any agreed contribution in person, in full.
  • Treat the car and everyone in it with respect.

Everyone

  • No harassment, discrimination, threats, or sexual content of any kind.
  • No commercial use — no advertising, no soliciting, no reselling seats.
  • No sharing another member's phone number, photo, or meetup details outside the ride.
  • No fake profiles, borrowed accounts, or pretending to be someone else.
  • Adults must not attempt to arrange private contact with a minor outside the platform.

For parents and guardians

If your child has a CarBuddy account, link to it. Until you do, they cannot join or post anything — that is enforced by the database, not by a hidden button.

1

Your child opens the Parent / Guardian page and generates a linking code.

2

You create your own account, open the same page, and enter their code.

3

Every ride request they send now waits for your approval, with the driver's name, rating, verification, route, time, and contribution shown before you decide.
