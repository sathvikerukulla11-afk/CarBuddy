How it works

Four steps, no guesswork

CarBuddy is for trips people are already taking. Nobody is dispatched, and nobody drives for money.

1Someone posts a trip

A driver already heading somewhere adds their route, date, time, and how many seats are genuinely free.

2You search nearby

Browse every current ride, or filter to pickups within a few miles of you. Tap Request to Join — you never join automatically.

3The driver decides

They see your profile, rating, and groups, then accept or decline. Under-18 riders also need guardian approval.

4You meet and travel

Once accepted you get the meetup point and contact details, and the seat count drops by one.

Seat availability

The seat count is always the truth

Seats are counted by the database, not the page you're looking at. Every accepted rider decrements the count inside a locked transaction, so two people can never take the same last seat — even if they tap at the same instant.

When a ride reaches zero, the request button disables itself and the server refuses any further joins. There is no state where a ride looks open but isn't.

Safety

Built for people who already have something in common

CarBuddy is not a service for putting children in cars with strangers. It is a way for a community that already knows each other to coordinate trips they're taking anyway — with adult oversight where it matters.

✓

Verified accounts

Members confirm their details and submit them for review. Verification status is visible on every ride card and profile.

👪

Guardian approval

Riders under 18 must have a linked parent or guardian, and every single request waits for that adult's decision before a driver can accept.

👥

Trusted groups

Restrict a ride to your school, team, or neighbourhood so only people inside that community can see or request it.

📍

Public meetup points

We ask for a public, well-lit meeting place instead of a home address, and only share it once a seat is confirmed.

⭐

Ratings that mean something

Only people who actually shared a completed ride can rate each other, once each. No brigading, no fake reviews.

🛡️

Report and block

Block someone and you disappear from each other's search results entirely. Reports go to a real moderation queue.

Trusted groups

Ride with your actual community

A trusted group is a real-world community: a school, a neighbourhood, a sports team, a club. Join one with a code, get approved by its admin, and you unlock rides that are only visible to that group.

Drivers choose per ride whether it's open to any verified member, limited to a group, or completely unlisted and shared by link only.
