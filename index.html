How it works

Four simple steps.

1

Someone shares a trip

A driver already going somewhere adds their route, time, and the seats genuinely free.

2

You find them

Browse rides near you, or filter to pickups within a few miles.

3

You request a seat

Your driver reviews your profile and decides. Under-18 riders need a guardian's approval too.

4

You travel together

Once confirmed you get the meetup point and contact details, and the seat count updates.

Seat availability

The seat count is always honest.

Seats are counted by the database, not by the page you're looking at. Every confirmed rider updates the count inside a locked transaction, so two people can never take the same last seat.

When a ride fills up it says so, and the server stops accepting requests. There is no state where a ride looks open but isn't.
